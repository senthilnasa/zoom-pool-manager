### Verification Checklist:
- [x] Feature: `Tests\Feature\RecurringSeriesTest`
  - [x] **RRULE Expansion & Blackout Skipping**: Evaluates recurrence rules (e.g., `FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=MO,WE,FR;COUNT=12`) and automatically detects and skips dates falling on organization or department `blackout_periods` (holidays, exams, maintenance) with annotated reason metadata.
  - [x] **Occurrence Capping**: Strictly enforces `MAX_OCCURRENCES = 50` per series to protect against runaway unbounded recurrence schedules.
  - [x] **Single Resource Series Allocation (`SINGLE_RESOURCE`)**: Validates candidate pool resources against all non-blackout dates in the series. Successfully assigns the identical Zoom resource across all occurrences when available and holds atomic reservations in `resource_reservations`.
  - [x] **Conflict Detection on Recurring Series**: When candidate resources have a conflict on any single occurrence date, series creation in `SINGLE_RESOURCE` mode is rejected with a descriptive error directing the user to `SPLIT_WHEN_NEEDED` or `PER_OCCURRENCE`.
  - [x] **Split & Per-Occurrence Series Allocation (`SPLIT_WHEN_NEEDED` / `PER_OCCURRENCE`)**: Optimizes resource assignment independently per occurrence date, distributing load across pool members.
  - [x] **Occurrence Detachment**: Allows detaching an individual occurrence from the parent series (`is_detached_from_series = true`). Detached occurrences can be rescheduled, reallocated to a different resource, or cancelled independently without breaking or mutating the remaining series occurrences.
  - [x] **Whole Series Cancellation**: Cancelling a series sets `series.status = 'cancelled'`, cancels all future unstarted occurrences, and releases their reservations in `resource_reservations`.
  - [x] **Web UI Management**: Series index (`/series`), creation wizard with interactive RRULE generator (`/series/create`), and series occurrences detail page (`/series/{publicId}`) render cleanly with full status tracking.

## 2. Manual Verification Walkthrough

### Step 1: Create a Recurring Series with Blackout Skipping
1. Log in to ZPM and navigate to **Recurring Series** (`/series`).
2. Click **New Recurring Series** (`/series/create`).
3. Enter Title (e.g. *"Physics 301 Lab"*), select Frequency **Weekly**, pick days (e.g. **Mon, Wed, Fri**), set Occurrences Count to **10**, and select Allocation Mode **Single Resource**.
4. Submit the form.
5. Verify redirect to the series details page (`/series/{publicId}`).
6. Observe all expanded occurrences with assigned resource badges. Note that any date overlapping an active holiday is excluded or skipped automatically.

### Step 2: Detach an Occurrence from the Series
1. On the series details page, locate Occurrence #2.
2. Click **Detach** in the actions column and confirm the prompt.
3. Observe redirect to the individual meeting page (`/meetings/{publicId}`).
4. Verify the meeting shows the **Detached** badge and can now be modified, rescheduled, or cancelled independently.
5. Return to `/series/{publicId}` and verify that the other occurrences remained untouched.

### Step 3: Cancel Entire Series
1. From `/series/{publicId}`, click **Cancel Series**.
2. Enter the cancellation reason in the modal and submit.
3. Verify series status updates to **Cancelled**, all future occurrences show **Cancelled** status, and their reservations in `resource_reservations` are marked `released`.

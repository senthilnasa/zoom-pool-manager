# Live Attendance & Participation

For academic grading, accreditation compliance, and corporate telemetry, Zoom Pool Manager captures live meeting attendance data (`/app/attendance`).

---

## 📊 Captured Attendance Metrics

When a meeting concludes, ZPM ingests participant telemetry through Zoom Past Meeting reports:
- **Participant Name & Email**: Identified institutional user or guest alias.
- **Join & Leave Timestamps**: Exact millisecond-level connect and disconnect times.
- **Duration (Minutes)**: Total time spent in the session.
- **Attentiveness & Device Info**: Platform (Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, Web).

---

## 📈 Aggregated Class Reports

Instructors and department leads can view:
- **Attendance Percentage**: Calculated against total scheduled class duration.
- **Late Arrivals & Early Exits**: Visual indicators highlighting students joining late.
- **CSV / Excel Export**: 1-click export formatted for LMS gradebooks (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard).
